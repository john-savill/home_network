Here’s a well-balanced **2-day weekend itinerary for Bath**, blending history, culture, relaxation, and local charm.

---

### **Day 1: Classic Bath – History & Architecture**
**Theme:** *Explore Bath’s UNESCO-listed heart and iconic landmarks*

**Morning:**
- **9:00 AM – Breakfast at The Ivy Bath**
  - A chic café near the Roman Baths, offering a great spread (try their avocado toast or full English).
  *OR* **The Olive Branch** (cozy spot with a warm welcome; book ahead).

- **10:00 AM – Roman Baths & Bath Abbey**
  - **Roman Baths** (book tickets in advance for 9:30 AM slots to avoid crowds).
  - **Bath Abbey**: Climb the tower for panoramic views (or visit the free chapel).
  - *Tip:* Combine with **Pulteney Bridge** (iconic Tudor-style bridge) on your way.

**Afternoon:**
- **12:30 PM – Lunch at The Salt Cellar** (Michelin-starred fine dining with a seasonal menu) *OR* **Gordon Ramsay’s The London** (rooftop views, modern British).

- **1:30 PM – The Jane Austen Centre** (20-min walk or short taxi ride)
  - Learn how Bath inspired *Pride and Prejudice* (free entry, fun quizzes!).
  - *Alternative:* **The Museum of Bath at Work** (local history, artisan workshops).

**Evening:**
- **6:00 PM – Dinner at The Olive Branch** (vegetarian-friendly) *OR* **The Royal Crescent** (uplifted modern British).
- **7:30 PM – Sunset at Pulteney Bridge & River Avon Walk**
  - Stroll along the river, then head to **The Holburne Museum** (art & history; free entry).

---

### **Day 2: Scenic & Local Bath**
**Theme:** *Off-the-beaten-path Bath with nature and local flavors*

**Morning:**
- **9:00 AM – Breakfast at The Olive Branch** (again) or **The Gainsborough** (coffee + croissants near the Circus).

- **10:00 AM – Bath’s Parks & Water Features**
  - **Bath Roman Baths gardens** (relaxing stroll with thermal pools).
  - **Weston Park** (free entry; try the **Wisteria Walk** in spring).
  - *OR* **Bath Sketching Circle** (guided walk for art lovers).

**Afternoon:**
- **12:30 PM – Lunch at The Ivy Bath** (try their chocolate soufflé!) or **The Pig at the Circus** (British pub with a great pie platter).
- **1:30 PM – Hidden Gems**
  - **The Paragon Buildings** (Georgian architecture; great for photos).
  - **The Assembly Rooms** (historic venue; free entry, events year-round).
  - *Alternative:* **The Sally Lunn House** (historic tea room; try their famous bun).

**Evening:**
- **6:00 PM – Sunset at North Gate Fields** (quiet, picturesque spot).
- **7:30 PM – Dinner at The White Horse** (traditional pub with local ales) *OR* **Mint Café** (vegan/vegetarian by-day, rooftop dining by-night).
- **8:30 PM – Drinks at The George Hotel** (historic pub) or **The Olive Branch’s cocktail bar**.

---

### **Extra Tips:**
- **Transport:** Bath’s city center is walkable, but taxis are useful for remote spots (e.g., Weston Park).
- **Tickets:** Book **Roman Baths** and **Jane Austen Centre** tickets online in advance.
- **Seasonal Add-ons:**
  - **Spring/Summer:** Bath Royal Baths & West Country Railway (scenic train ride).
  - **Winter:** Cozy up at **The George** or **The Ivy Bath’s rooftop bar** (winter events).
