# Weekend Itinerary: A Bath Escape

**Location:** Bath, Somerset, UK
**Duration:** One full weekend (6 days)
**Theme:** Bath is a city of steam, history, and stone. This itinerary blends the ancient Roman grandeur with the modern, atmospheric pubs and the iconic bathhouse experience.

*Note: Please check current opening hours for Bath's famous Bathhouse and the City of Bath Museum, as some may require booking in advance.*

---

## Day 1: The Roman Heart of the City
**Theme:** History & Architecture

*   **Morning:** **The Roman Baths (Kensal Green)**.
    *   Start early to avoid crowds. The thermal pool is the highlight; feel the heat and soak off your worries.
    *   *Tip:* Don't buy your ticket at the ticket office; buy it online beforehand (check the official Bath Council website for current entry times).
*   **Lunch:** Head to **The Great Western Market** or sit at a table in a Victorian pub like **The King's Arms** for a hearty, traditional English meal.
*   **Afternoon:** Walk through the **Market Garden** area or the **Bath Abbey** grounds (if you have time).
*   **Evening:** **The Bath House** (The world's oldest public bathhouse).
    *   *Must-visit:* The **Maidstone** and **Sutton** baths. These are famous for their steam rooms, which can be surprisingly hot.
    *   *Budget Tip:* Grab a quick lunch at a nearby pub before heading in; the baths can be a long day.
*   **Night:** **Cathedral Square**. Stroll down the Promenade, visit the **Bath Abbey** clock tower, and enjoy the famous "Cathedral Square" beer gardens.

## Day 2: The Spa and River Life
**Theme:** Relaxation & Nature

*   **Morning:** **The Spa**.
    *   Choose either the **Bath Spa** (the main, historic facility) or the **Bath Spa Hotel Spa** (more modern, closer to the city). The Bath Spa is the legendary 3,000-year-old Roman site; the hotel spa offers a more comfortable, contemporary experience.
*   **Afternoon:** Visit the **Bath Spa Castle**.
    *   Explore the medieval ramparts, the Gothic chapel, and the **Bath Spa Castle Museum**. It offers a fascinating look at the city's medieval past.
*   **Late Afternoon:** Relax by the **River Avon**.
    *   Take a boat ride if the weather permits (the river is very calm here), or simply sit in the riverbank to watch the boats move.
*   **Evening:** Dinner at **The Duke of York's House**.
    *   This is a world-class restaurant in the heart of Bath. It's slightly more formal and expensive, but the food is exceptional and the ambiance is luxurious.
*   **Night:** Walk around **Wells Street** (the main shopping street) to see the local shops and cafes before heading out.

## Day 3: The City & The Waterfront
**Theme:** Culture & Views

*   **Morning:** **The City of Bath Museum**.
    *   Open daily (9:30 am – 12:30 pm & 2:30 pm – 4:30 pm). It's a great place to see the original Roman baths, the Victorian architecture, and the museum shop.
*   **Lunch:** **Bath Market**.
    *   This is a culinary gem. You can enjoy a full market lunch or grab a quick sandwich at a nearby stall. The food here is excellent and very British.
*   **Afternoon:** **The Avon Grotto**.
    *   Located on the banks of the River Avon, this is one of the most photographed spots in the UK. You can walk along the promenade, enjoy the view of the city, and take photos. It feels like stepping back in time.
*   **Late Afternoon:** Visit the **Bath Abbey** again or take a walk up to the **St Paul's Hill** for a panoramic view of the city.
*   **Evening:** **The Royal Victoria Opera House** (The Royal Victoria Opera).
    *   This is the largest opera house in the UK. It is famous for its stunning architecture (built by the Duke of Devonshire). You can enjoy a private concert, a gala, or simply sit and watch the show.
*   **Night:** Enjoy a **River Boat Cruise**.
    *   Take a guided boat cruise across the River Avon. It offers great views of the city lights and is a perfect way to end the day.

## Day 4: Day Trip (Option A: Stonehenge or Option B: Blandford Forum)
**Theme:** Adventure & Nature

*   **Option A (Classic):** Drive north to **Stonehenge**.
    *   Spend the day exploring the ancient monument and the surrounding **Blandford Forum**. The views of the city from the top of Blandford Forum are magnificent.
    *   *Note:* Drive takes about 1 hour from the city.
*   **Option B (City Escape):** Head south to **Blandford Forum**.
    *   Visit the **Blandford Forum Museum** and the **Blandford Forum Castle**.
    *   Take a short walk to the **River Avon Grotto** again, or simply enjoy the peaceful village atmosphere before heading back.
    *   *Why do this:* To get away from the city center and experience the countryside charm of the Somerset Levels.

## Day 5: Culture & The Market
**Theme:** Art & History

*   **Morning:** **The Royal Victoria Opera House**.
    *   Enjoy the show! If you missed it on Day 3, it's always worth a second look.
*   **Afternoon:** **Bath Market** (again).
    *   It's best to visit in the afternoon when the market stalls are still active. Grab fresh bread, fish and chips, and cakes.
*   **Late Afternoon:** Walk around **Bath Abbey** or take a stroll through **Bath House Gardens**.
    *   The gardens are beautiful and offer a peaceful break from the city noise.
*   **Evening:** **The King's Arms** or **The Royal Victoria**.
    *   Pick up a pint, enjoy the city life, and maybe grab a quick walk around the market before dinner.

## Day 6: Relaxation & Departure
**Theme:** The Final Stretch

*   **Morning:** Choose **The Royal Victoria Opera** for a final time.
*   **Late Morning:** **Bath Abbey**.
    *   Spend the morning inside. It's quiet and allows you to reflect on the history you've seen.
*   **Late Afternoon:** Lunch at **The Great Western Market** for a final market feast (or a nearby pub).
*   **Evening:** **The Queen's House** (if you have time) or just a final walk around **Cathedral Square** before heading home.
*   **Depart:** Leave the city and enjoy your own way home.

---

### Essential Tips for Bath

1.  **The Bath House:** The Bath House is a world heritage site. The **Maidstone** and **Sutton** baths are the most famous; the **Llandaff** bath is slightly more affordable and famous. You can book tickets online weeks in advance.
2.  **Pubs:** Bath is famous for its pubs. Almost every corner has one. Try to visit one every afternoon for a proper British dinner and a drink.
3.  **Weather:** It is a coastal climate. The weather can be cold and windy at night, even in summer. Bring a light jacket and a scarf if it's raining.
4.  **Transport:** Bath has a fantastic bus network (Babes and Boots). The **City Loop** bus service runs around Cathedral Square, though the route is changing; always check the current map before you go.
5.  **Parks:** There are two main parks: **Bath House Gardens** and **Cathedral Square**. Both are beautiful, but they are different.

Enjoy your time in the City of Bath! It is a truly magical place.
